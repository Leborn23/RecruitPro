## Style Prompt
RecruitPro promo should feel like an enterprise recruiting SaaS for HR leaders: bright, precise, calm, and operationally credible. Use a light canvas with deep blue structure, generous whitespace, soft glass surfaces, and restrained motion that communicates flow instead of hype. The video should look like a premium product demo, not a consumer ad.

## Colors
- `#F3F7FC` background canvas
- `#0F172A` primary text and structural contrast
- `#1F6FEB` brand blue for accents and key action states
- `#7DD3FC` secondary highlight glow
- `#11895E` positive decision/status accent

## Typography
- `Manrope` for Latin UI and headline rhythm
- `Noto Sans SC` for Chinese copy support

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use neon gradients, purple tech palettes, or dark mode
- Do not over-animate screenshots with gimmicky spins or zooms
- Do not center everything; preserve strong editorial hierarchy
- Do not use generic startup slogans or empty productivity claims
- Do not make the product UI look fictional when real screenshots are available
